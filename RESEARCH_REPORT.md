## 1. Executive Summary

Glitter Bean Café is a worker-owned, unionized cooperative café located at **5896 Spring Garden Road** in Halifax, Nova Scotia. Established in July 2018 by unionized baristas (SEIU Local 2) following the closure of the Smiling Goat Organic Espresso Bar, Glitter Bean operates as Atlantic Canada's first unionized worker-cooperative café and a premier queer-, trans-, and Two-Spirit-affirming community hub. The café brews fair-trade and organic coffee exclusively from **Just Us! Coffee Roasters Co-operative** (who also own the historic Victorian building) and partners with local Nova Scotia bakers including **Tart & Soul**, **Schoolhouse Gluten-Free Gourmet**, and **Staff of Life**.
