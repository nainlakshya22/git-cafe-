Support / FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Glitter Bean Café Co-op in Halifax.

Ask a question
Where are you located?

We're at 5896 Spring Garden Road, Halifax, NS B3H 0A6 — right on the Spring Garden Road strip in the heart of Halifax.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday: 7:30am–6:00pm
Saturday: 10:00am–5:00pm
Sunday: 11:00am–5:00pm (Dry Sunday — alcohol-free)

What coffee do you use?

We proudly serve fair-trade, organic coffee from Just Us! Coffee Roasters Co-op — a pioneering Nova Scotia co-operative committed to ethical sourcing.

What is the Gem Program?

The Gem Program is our pay-it-forward initiative. You can purchase a Gem token at the counter, and someone in need can redeem it for a hot drink or snack. It's a small act of community solidarity.

Are you worker-owned?

Yes! Glitter Bean Café Co-op was founded in July 2018 as Atlantic Canada's first unionized worker co-operative café. We are owned and operated by our workers.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Yes! We offer dairy-free milk alternatives (oat, soy, almond) and can accommodate many dietary needs. Our menu includes vegan and gluten-free options. Ask our staff for details.

Do you have Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for customers. Ask our baristas for the network details.

Can I host events at Glitter Bean?

We occasionally host community events! Check our Eventbrite page for upcoming events, or contact us to discuss hosting.

Do you do catering?

For catering inquiries, please contact us directly at glitterbeancafe@gmail.com or call (902) 407-5715.

Is the café accessible?

We strive to be accessible. Contact us at (902) 407-5715 for specific accessibility information before your visit.

Is Glitter Bean queer-friendly?

Absolutely. Glitter Bean is a queer, trans, and Two-Spirit affirming community hub. Everyone is welcome here.
