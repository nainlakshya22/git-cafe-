FAQ

Helpful answers about coffee, subscriptions, pickup, and our café experience.

Ask a question

Do you offer pickup and local delivery?

Yes — local pickup is available in-store, and we can help with local delivery for select orders and café events.

Can I customize my subscription?

Absolutely. You can change roasts, delivery cadence, or skip a shipment at any time from your subscription account.

Do you have dairy-free or vegan options?

Yes. We offer oat, almond, and coconut milks, and many drinks can be adjusted to suit dietary needs.

Can I host an event at the café?

We welcome small community and business gatherings. Reach out to us to discuss space, timing, and service needs.
